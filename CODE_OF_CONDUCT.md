# Code of Conduct for Advanced Tab Manager Pro

The **Advanced Tab Manager Pro** community is dedicated to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for everyone, regardless of background, identity, or experience level. This Code of Conduct outlines our expectations for all participants and the consequences for unacceptable behavior.

## Our Pledge

We, as contributors and maintainers, pledge to make participation in our project and community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

## Expected Behavior

To foster a positive and collaborative environment, we expect all participants to:

- **Be Respectful**: Treat others with kindness, empathy, and respect.
- **Be Inclusive**: Welcome diverse perspectives and encourage participation from all community members.
- **Be Collaborative**: Provide constructive feedback and work together to improve the project.
- **Be Professional**: Communicate clearly and avoid personal attacks or derogatory language.
- **Follow Guidelines**: Adhere to the project’s [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/VoxDroid/Advanced-Tab-Manager/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) and [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/VoxDroid/Advanced-Tab-Manager/blob/main/SECURITY.md) guidelines.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are considered unacceptable and will not be tolerated:

- **Harassment**: Any form of harassment, including offensive comments, trolling, or personal attacks related to gender, sexual orientation, race, religion, or other personal characteristics.
- **Discrimination**: Discriminatory remarks or actions that exclude or marginalize individuals.
- **Inappropriate Content**: Sharing sexualized content, violent imagery, or other material that violates community standards.
- **Disruptive Behavior**: Trolling, spamming, or derailing discussions in Issues, Discussions, or other community spaces.
- **Privacy Violations**: Sharing private information about others without consent.
- **Other Unethical Conduct**: Any behavior that violates the spirit of this Code of Conduct or local laws.

## Reporting Violations

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it promptly:

- **Contact the Developer**:
  - Email: [izeno.contact@gmail.com](mailto:izeno.contact@gmail.com)
  - Subject: “Code of Conduct Violation: Advanced Tab Manager Pro”
  - Include a detailed description of the incident, including screenshots or logs if applicable.
- **GitHub Issues**: For non-sensitive violations, use the **General Inquiry** issue template on the [Issues](https://github.com/VoxDroid/Advanced-Tab-Manager/issues) page, but avoid including personal details.

Reports will be handled confidentially, and retaliation against reporters will not be tolerated.

## Enforcement

Violations of this Code of Conduct will be reviewed by the project maintainers. Consequences may include:

- **Warning**: A private or public warning to cease the behavior.
- **Temporary Ban**: Temporary suspension from participating in the project’s community spaces (e.g., Issues, Discussions).
- **Permanent Ban**: Permanent removal from the project’s community spaces.
- **Other Actions**: Additional measures as deemed appropriate to address the violation.

Maintainers reserve the right to remove, edit, or reject contributions (e.g., comments, issues, pull requests) that violate this Code of Conduct.
